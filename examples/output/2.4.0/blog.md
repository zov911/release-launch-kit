# Northwind v2.4.0: AI lead scoring and 8 more updates

Today we're releasing Northwind v2.4.0. The highlight: AI lead scoring that ranks every new HubSpot contact by fit and intent, plus 8 more updates. Here's what it means for B2B marketing and RevOps teams.

## What's new

### AI lead scoring

AI lead scoring that ranks every new HubSpot contact by fit and intent. Available now for all customers.

### Slack alerts

Slack alerts when a high-intent account visits the pricing page. Available now for all customers.

### Shareable dashboard links with view-only access

Available now for all customers.

### Bulk export endpoint supports CSV and Parquet

Available now for all customers.

## Breaking changes

- Legacy v1 REST API (deprecated since March). Migrate to v2

## Security

- Upgraded session tokens to rotate every 12 hours

## Improved

- Reports load up to 3x faster on accounts with more than 1M events

## Fixed

- Attribution totals no longer double-count leads that convert twice in one day
- Fixed timezone offset in weekly email digests

> **Before you upgrade:** this release includes 1 breaking change. Review them above and update your integration first.
